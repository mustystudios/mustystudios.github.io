Bandit Dash

A rooftop race through five worlds

Race two rival raccoons across glowing rooftops, sunset ruins, misty jungle cliffs, frozen peaks and a neon harbor. Tap to jump, swipe to slide and grab power-ups to stay ahead. Finish first to earn stars, coins and rare keys.

Coming soon to Google Play

Features

  • Five worlds, three stages each. Midnight Rooftops, Desert Ruins, Jungle Cliffs, Frozen Peaks and Neon Harbor, each with its own hazards.
  • Real racing. Chase two rivals to the finish line. Finish first for three stars.
  • Power-ups. Shield, magnet, jump boots, glider and double score.
  • Collect and customise. Unlock raccoon outfits and boot styles with the coins you earn.
  • Daily missions with coins and keys, and rewards for every race.
  • Light and fast. A small download that runs smoothly on most phones.
