Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information Bandit Dash and our website use, why we use it and the choices you have. It applies to the game Bandit Dash and to this website, both run by Musty Studios ("we", "us"), an independent studio based in Nigeria. Contact: mustythecreator@gmail.com.

In short:
  • Your progress, coins, keys and settings are stored on your phone.
  • We do not ask for your name, address, phone number or payment details.
  • Our ad partner (Google AdMob) and other Google services collect some technical data to show ads, process purchases, save your game and fix crashes. Details are below.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Information we collect

Stored on your device

Game progress, stars, high scores, coins, keys, purchased outfits, settings and reminder preferences are saved on your device. If you do not use cloud save, this data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising data (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

We show ads using Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location.
  • Interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google may use it to personalise ads where allowed. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy coins, keys or other items, Google Play processes the payment. We receive confirmation of the purchase (product and order details) so we can deliver your items. We never see your card or bank details.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides us with your player ID and public profile name and picture. We use this to save your game to the cloud and to show leaderboards. Saved game data is stored by Google.

Crash and performance reports

To fix bugs we collect crash reports with technical details such as device model, Android version, game version, the crash location and an installation identifier. These are processed by Google (Firebase Crashlytics).

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device. This does not send your data anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the game settings or in Android settings.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to reply.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and deliver purchases.
  • To show ads and keep the game free.
  • To detect fraud, cheating and abuse.
  • To find and fix crashes and improve performance.
  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Advertising choices

  • Ad ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in Settings, under Privacy, then Ads.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland: where required, the game shows a consent message through Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can change your choice later where the game offers it.
  • US state laws: where required, you can opt out of the sharing of your data for personalised advertising through Google's tools or by contacting us.

4. Legal bases (EU and UK users)

We rely on your consent for personalised ads and for the use of advertising identifiers; on performance of a contract to deliver the game and your purchases; on our legitimate interests to keep the game secure, prevent fraud and fix crashes; and on legal obligation where applicable.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information for money. Data is processed by service providers that help us run the game: Google (advertising, billing, Play Games and crash reporting). Some advertising sharing may count as "sharing" for advertising under certain US state laws, and you can opt out as described above. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety and rights of players and the game.

6. International transfers

Google and its partners process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. When data is transferred, these providers use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent protections required by applicable law.

7. How long we keep data

  • Game data on your device: until you uninstall the game or reset your progress.
  • Cloud saves: until you delete them in Google Play Games or ask us to remove them.
  • Advertising, billing and crash data: for periods set by Google under its own policies.
  • Emails to us: only as long as needed to handle your request.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to receive a copy, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. This includes rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. We will not treat you unfairly for using these rights.

To use your rights or delete your data: email mustythecreator@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request" and tell us what you need. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We aim to answer within 30 days. To delete data kept on your phone, uninstall the game. To delete a cloud save, remove it in the Google Play Games app.

9. Children

Bandit Dash is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country), please do not use the game without a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, including encrypted connections to our service providers and checks that help detect altered save data. No system is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game or on this site.

12. Contact
